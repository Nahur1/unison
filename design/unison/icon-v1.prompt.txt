Use case: logo-brand.
Create the final square desktop app icon for UNISON, an elegant music and video player with offline Persian subtitle translation. This is a completely new brand identity.
Single icon only, front-facing orthographic, no presentation board, no device, no text, no lettering other than the abstract U-shaped symbol. No Persian text.
Design an exceptionally simple memorable sculptural monogram: a continuous broad U-shaped ribbon, its right arm folding inward into a single crisp right-facing play triangle. The triangle must be structurally integrated with the U ribbon, with generous negative space; one cohesive mark rather than several pasted symbols. The U represents voices joining in unison; its curved lower stroke subtly evokes an audio wave and the folded end evokes playback and dialogue. Do not cram in additional music notes, captions, globes, sparkles, infinity signs or equalizer bars.
A carefully balanced macOS-style squircle tile fills about 88% of the square canvas, centered with equal margins. Tile is deep midnight graphite with soft bevel and refined satin finish. The central U/play mark is large, about 61% of canvas width, with thick generous strokes and rounded outer contours. Material is luminous frosted glass with restrained pearlescent silver, ice blue and pale lavender highlights, readable silhouette and softly shaded depth. Controlled studio lighting from upper left, extremely subtle shadow confined to tile, premium Apple-adjacent desktop aesthetic, calm rather than flashy, crisp at small sizes, no neon glow, no lens flare, no gratuitous ornament.
Actual transparent canvas outside the rounded-square tile; retain the tile's opaque midnight background. High-resolution production icon, precisely centered and isolated.
